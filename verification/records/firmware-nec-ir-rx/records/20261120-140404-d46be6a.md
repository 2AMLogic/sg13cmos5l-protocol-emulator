# firmware-nec-ir-rx — 20261120-140404-d46be6a

- **Record ID**: 20261120-140404-d46be6a
- **Claim**: The 115-word NEC receiver (`firmware/asm/nec_ir_rx.asm`) decodes the demodulating receiver module's envelope on `ui_in[4]`. For every well-formed frame driven by the independent model's encoder (converted to the receiver's envelope, idle high), the firmware writes the frame's address byte then its command byte to `uo_out` (`nec_ir_rx_result`, DR 0010) and nothing else; a repeat code, a bad-complement frame (either pair) and a truncated frame write nothing and leave the previous result intact, and the decoder resynchronises after every discard. The 40,781-cycle mid-space sample decodes every frame on the model's ±10% `TIMING_TOL_PCT` window edges (including the razor combination space0 and bit_mark both −10%, and all three widths together at each edge); a documented-accepted −15% `space0_ns` frame still decodes; a ×1.5-stretched frame does not come back correct.
- **Design provenance**: RTL and the 115-word image at `d46be6aa234d`. The source hashes above identify the RTL, image, assembler, model and bench.
- **Run configuration**: `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-nec-ir-rx.json --format json`. The simulation clock period is 20 ns (target-spec row 4's unconfirmed 50 MHz; the claim is the cycle count). No gate-level run is claimed.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed tests with seed 1. Frames: the committed 0xA5/0x3C pair, 0x00/0xFF and 0x8D/13; a seeded random pair (31/42, C3/96); every ±10% edge combination; the −15% and ×1.5 controls. Writes are graded as `uo_out` change events per frame window, against the driven bytes; every graded frame was first proven legal by the model's own decoder.
- **Result**: **PASS, 7/7** (0.289–1.503 s sim time per test). The KLT envelope and Icarus results XML in `artifacts/20261120-140404-d46be6a/` contain the individual results.
