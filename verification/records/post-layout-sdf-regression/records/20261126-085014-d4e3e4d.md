- **Statistical convention**: At each fixed corner and rate, the bench decoded two 8N1 frames using the programmed bit period T. For timestamps spanning Δt from the falling start edge to the last transition at bit index k, the mean period is Δt/k and the drift is 100 × |Δt − kT| / T. The last edge is at k = 9 for 0x5a and k = 8 for 0xa5.
