# Routed UART timing and framing-error replay

- **Record ID**: 20261126-061939-2dca74c
- **Claim**: At the 20 ns SDC clock, all 18 measured UART TX frames met the 2% last-edge drift limit. The wrapper preserved the raw measurements from all three images when a diagnostic replay caused the stretched timing control to raise a framing error.
- **Design provenance**: Harness revision `2dca74c8fe83337daf06af2bba73e933dbb2655b`, the routed netlist and SDC from [GDS run 38136727636](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13cmos5l-protocol-emulator/actions/runs/38136727636), and the IHP standard-cell and UDP models identified in the metadata. The netlist SHA-256 is `fbc7bc30a4987aaa47ea2633df46993ebe1ee42171edc54173e25ff343e085ba`. The SDC SHA-256 is `b1eebe0bdede7bfba2f181eae451cc80ea7ab71e41ade10ba138782cc0cab4c2`.
- **Run configuration**: `flow/run-post-layout-sdf.sh --suite protocols --from <frozen-inputs> nom_typ_1p20V_25C nom_slow_1p08V_125C nom_fast_1p32V_m40C` with Icarus 13.0, cocotb 2.1.0 and seed 1. The runner derived the 20 ns clock and 4 ns input and output delays from the SDC. The archive contains the requests, klt envelopes, XML results, simulator logs, per-pin reports, normalization reports and aggregate grade. Local path prefixes use placeholders. `original-sha256.json` lists the hashes before path substitution.
- **Statistical convention**: Two directed payloads per image per corner. For the ordered pin timestamps of each decoded frame, Δt is the time from the first edge to the last. The last transition occurs at bit index k = 9 for `0x5a` and k = 8 for `0xa5`. The mean period is Δt/k. Drift is 100 × |Δt − kT| / T, where T is the image's programmed bit period.
- **Result**: All nine annotated protocol/corner cells passed with zero pin-contract violations. At each corner UART passed 4/4, SPI passed 3/3, and I²C repeated START passed 5/5. The timestamps reproduce the reported period, baud and drift of every UART frame. All nine 1.025× controls decoded both bytes and rejected both frames on timing. The greatest nominal drift was 0.0010% of one bit period.

**Excursion replays.** Scaling the typical-corner 1 Mbaud capture's timestamps by 1.04 yielded 36.0010% drift for `0x5a` and 32.0010% for `0xa5`. Both measured frames decoded with valid stop bits. Applying the additional 1.025× control produced `framing error at t=67227.230ns: stop bit sampled 0`. The saved JSON retained both frames' edges and drift, the control error, and the four frames from the slower images before the bench failed. The archive's `replay/` directory also contains the nominal, 1.025× and 0.975× replays. The latter two retained all six frames with maximum drift of 22.5010% and 22.5000% respectively.

**Timing coverage.** Icarus reports partial SDF annotation, including 4,443 dropped conditional IOPATH entries in each cell. The runner removes the SRAM timing block and uses its functional model. The measurement ends at the macro pin. RX sampling, other TX rates, pads and package timing remain outside this result. Row 10 retains its failing grade. The earlier pre-fix I²C wiring control is documented in the predecessor chain.
