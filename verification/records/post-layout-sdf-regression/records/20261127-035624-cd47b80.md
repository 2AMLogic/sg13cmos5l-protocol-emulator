# Routed UART TX timing at the inclusive drift limit and after a framing error

- **Record ID**: 20261127-035624-cd47b80
- **Claim**: At the 20 ns SDC clock, the last-edge drift of 18 directed UART TX frames on the routed macro output is at most 0.0010% of one programmed bit. The saved report accepts an excursion of exactly 2% and measures a valid second frame after a framing error.
- **Design provenance**: The measurement wrapper and replay tests ran at `cd47b8006003e48638cbfe70a1c9de97d5b8752d`. The frozen netlist and SDC from [GDS run 38136727636](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13cmos5l-protocol-emulator/actions/runs/38136727636) have SHA-256 `fbc7bc30a4987aaa47ea2633df46993ebe1ee42171edc54173e25ff343e085ba` and `b1eebe0bdede7bfba2f181eae451cc80ea7ab71e41ade10ba138782cc0cab4c2`, respectively.

- **Statistical convention**: Two directed 8N1 frames per image at each corner. For elapsed time Δt between first and last edges, last-edge bit index k, and programmed bit period T, mean period is Δt/k and drift is 100 × |Δt − kT|/T. The payloads 0x5A and 0xA5 place their last rising edges at k = 9 and k = 8.
- **Result**: All nine SDF-annotated runs passed with zero pin-contract violations. Each corner passed four UART, three SPI and five I²C tests. Signed UART residuals were +10 ps at typical, −10 ps at slow and 0 ps at fast. At 1.025× stretch, all frames decoded and exceeded the 2% limit. Four zero-delay RTL tests passed, with zero drift in each directed frame.
