- **Record ID**: 20261014-123000-a602461
- **Claim**: **Margin of the CPHA=1 functional-burst `IN` against the peripheral's clock-to-MISO delay (issue #309, DR 0021 open item 1).** In SPI modes 1 and 3 the leading-edge `OUT` is where the peripheral launches MISO and the trailing-edge `OUT` is where it is sampled. Before this change the functional burst's `IN` was instruction 2 of the 10-instruction bit: 1 core cycle after the launch edge (5 cycles before the sampling edge). It is now instruction 5: 4 core cycles after the launch edge and 1 cycle before the trailing `OUT` (the extraction `AND`/`SHF`/`SHF` follows the trailing edge; the previous bit's `SHF R0`/`OR` fill the slots after the leading edge; bit 7's pair runs after the section, inside the CS-hold cycles). Every `OUT` is at its original cycle; the 80-cycle section, 10 cycles/bit, 198 total cycles and the `WORDS 138 -> 140` change are the whole cycle-report diff outside the moved instructions. **Measured, with the peripheral's MISO change delayed by whole core cycles (the change lands 1 ns after the clock edge it is delayed to; four byte patterns 0x5A, 0x00, 0xFF, 0xA5; modes 1 and 3):** the new images receive every pattern at delays 0, 1, 2 and 3 cycles and miss at 4; the pre-#309 images (kept byte for byte as `verification/fixtures/spi_mode{1,3}_early_in.asm`) receive every pattern at delay 0 and miss 0x5A, 0x00 and 0xA5 at delays 1, 2 and 3 (the negative control that makes the sweep able to fail on the old placement). **Margin statement.** At the nominal 20 ns core period (target-spec row 4's >= 50 MHz Target, `spec/target-spec.md` row 4; the bench's `CLK_PERIOD_NS = 20`; the same 20 ns as `src/config.json` `CLOCK_PERIOD`, which closes post-route on the LibreLane flow at 3 corners while row 4 stays unmet and the klt flow has no result): the old placement tolerated a peripheral MISO change less than 21 ns after the launch edge (between 1 ns and 21 ns, bench resolution of one cycle); the new placement tolerates 61 ns and fails at 81 ns, i.e. a clock-to-MISO (plus board and input path) delay of up to 3 cycles = 60 ns, with the true boundary between 61 and 81 ns. At row 4's 100 MHz Stretch (10 ns) the same 3 cycles are 30 ns. The peripheral t_V is an ASSUMPTION, not a datasheet value: no peripheral part is selected in this repo, so the class assumed is a serial peripheral at 3.3 V with a clock-to-output valid time of up to 50 ns including the board; against that class the old placement (< 21 ns) fails and the new one (>= 61 ns) passes at 50 MHz, while at 100 MHz the new one's 30 ns floor would not cover the top of that class (a faster clock needs the stated t_V to be re-read against a chosen part). The ns figures are arithmetic at an UNCONFIRMED clock: the cycle counts are the measurement.
