  `firmware/asm/spi_mode{0..3}.asm`, images `firmware/build/spi_mode*.hex`, one

  DR 0001's ceiling, 2-cycle half-periods, zero `WAIT`, the MISO sample `IN`

  the received byte and echoes it on `uo_out[7:0]` after CS releases; and the
  **independent** reference model `verification/reference_models/spi.py` —
  which shares no code with this repo's RTL or firmware and encodes only the
  Motorola/Freescale/NXP "SPI Block Guide" mode conventions ratified
  target-spec row 11 names — grades **both bursts conformant in all four
  modes** (CPOL idle levels, CS envelope, SCLK ≤ f_clk/4 at the named core
  clock, `sample-edge count` whole-byte), re-deriving **both data streams

  `0xC3`/`0x5A` as driven by the bench's independent peripheral coroutine, and

  it is what `CLAUDE.md`'s "firmware is evidence" requires of a protocol claim:
  a committed program, run on the RTL under cocotb, passing against an
  independent reference model of that protocol with the timing checked.

    modes, at the pin.** Measured two ways that agree: the model's ns-domain
    measurement (80.000 ns, uniform +1 ps capture offsets cancel exactly) and
    a cycle-domain measurement done by the bench from captured edge indices —
    every one of the 15 SCLK half-period gaps inside each ceiling burst
    window is exactly 2 clock edges (and exactly 4 in each functional burst
    window), so the numbers carry no clock-rate assumption. Row 11's ceiling
    itself is verified at the fastest legal rate, not only slower rates: 4
    cycles is the floor the ISA's 1-cycle instructions permit (2 OUTs per
    bit), i.e. the burst is at the ceiling, not merely under it.

    program then echoes on `uo_out[7:0]` — the bench observes the echo land

    modes. Peripheral side: the MISO coroutine is independent of the
    program's internals — it reacts only to observed CS/SCLK pins, presenting
    bits per the model's own `encode_burst` convention (CPHA=0: bit 0 at CS
    fall, bit k after trailing edge k−1; CPHA=1: bit k at leading edge k),
    and `check_burst` independently confirms what it drove by re-deriving it
    from the captured pin.

  - **ISA finding (dated 2026-10-02, per #72 acceptance criterion 5): DR
    0001's SPI cycle-budget sketch holds at the budget level; its literal
    instruction sketch does not.** The sketch's claim — 4 cycles/bit at the
    ceiling (SCLK = f_clk/4, 2-cycle phases, zero `WAIT`) **with the MISO
    sample inside the loop** — is **confirmed against the real core**: 4
    cycles/bit measured at the pin, sample included, all four modes. But the
    sketch's four instructions are not writable in this ISA as sketched: its
    single `SHF Rshift, left` is commented "shift in sampled bit, shift out
    next TX bit", which presumes a shift that merges two registers and
    advances a port-image byte through pin positions — the real `SHF` shifts
    one register by 1, fills with 0, writes only `C` (readable by nothing
    but branches' kin — no ADC/rotate-through-carry exists), and there is no
    immediate ALU form (`AND`/`OR`/`XOR` are register-register only; DR 0001
    deliberately has no `SUBI`/`ADDI`). A port image carrying CS+SCLK+MOSI
    cannot hold a shifting data byte (10 bits needed, 8 available), so the
    data-bearing phase write is built from per-bit `LDI` immediates (the
    payload is a committed constant, so this costs nothing but program
    words), and the other phase's write is a static register image.
    **Receive-side assembly does not fit at 4 cycles/bit** (`IN` + `AND`
    extract + `SHF`/`OR` accumulate = 4 more ops/bit on top of the 2 OUTs +
    1 IN + 1 data op), which is why the functional burst runs at 8 cycles/bit.
    This is a *finding about the sketch's instruction-level detail*, not an
    ISA defect: no opcode, register or RTL change was needed or wanted, and
    the ratified budget (the number the sketch exists to justify) is
    confirmed. Whether a future ISA revision wants a merge-shift or
    immediate-ALU form to close the assembly gap is a DR-level question,
    out of scope here and unrecorded as anything but this note.
  - **No MHz figure is measured, and no ns figure here is a measurement of
    frequency.** Target-spec row 4 (≥50 MHz core clock) is **unconfirmed on
