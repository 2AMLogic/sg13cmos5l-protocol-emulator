- **Claim**: The seeded constrained-random DUT regression (seed `20261009`, 20 programs each of UART TX, SPI, I2C write and I2C write/repeated-START/read with seeded clock stretching; verification-plan § 4, target-spec row 8) passes against the independent models with **both I2C families on lines the design drives through its real `uio_oe`**. The 40 generated I2C programs run on the silicon-true pad model `verification/uio_pads.py` (per `uio` pin: the design's `uio_oe`/`uio_out`, a pull-up on SCL = `uio[0]` and SDA = `uio[7]`, the peripheral as an external open-drain driver, resolved into one line that `uio_in` reads; the six other lines tied low), through the directed benches' own `run_words` / `run_on_pads`; the model grades SCL and SDA as sampled on `uio_in`; and each of the 40 must end having enabled exactly the SCL and SDA pads, only ever driven them low, and never contended. The UART and SPI families are unchanged (dedicated outputs; no pad model involved). **Why this record supersedes.** The superseded record's bus was composed by the bench: `line = uio_out AND uio_in`, with `uio_oe` never read (DR 0008 § Context). That composition is what an open-drain bus does *if* the pads implement open-drain, so it could not distinguish a chip that drives the line from one that cannot -- and it did not: the same composition passed on the pre-DR-0012 top, whose `uio_oe` was `8'h00` and which could not pull an I2C line low. The superseded record said as much ("no evidence that the pins are driven correctly ... pads are issue #136"). Its *numbers* are not corrected here -- they reproduce -- its *basis* is replaced: DR 0012 § Consequences and `spec/verification-plan.md` § 7 require the I2C evidence to run on a silicon-true pad model, and this run does. Same scope boundary otherwise: UART RX deferred, RTL only. **What this record does not claim.** The pad model is zero-delay and logical: nothing here is evidence about pull-up rise time, pad propagation delay, drive strength or bus capacitance, and an I2C bus still needs external pull-ups this design does not provide. DR 0012 is **Proposed, not ratified**; this record makes no target-spec row met.
