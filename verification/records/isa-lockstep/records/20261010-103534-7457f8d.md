- **Record ID**: 20261010-103534-7457f8d
- **Claim**: The core (`rtl/protocol_core.v`, through `src/tt_um_2amlogic_protocol_emulator.v`) executes DR 0001's 16 opcodes and DR 0012's control space (`WCTL`/`RCTL` on `UIO_DIR`, `UIO_OD`, `PM_ADDR`, `PM_DATA_HI/LO`, `PM_CRC_LO/HI`, `BOOT_STATUS`, `HW_ID`, `RUN`, with their fixed 1- or 2-cycle costs, the reserved no-op encodings and the unassigned / read-only / write-only index behaviour) exactly as an independent, table-driven instruction-set simulator written from those two records says, on every clock edge of 400 seeded terminating programs (27,983 edges compared). Compared per edge: `pc`, `R0`-`R3`, `Z`, `C`, `halted` (hierarchy into `dut.u_core`, RTL only) and `uo_out` / `uio_out` / `uio_oe` (pins), including each stall edge of `WAIT n` (n+1 edges), each two-cycle control access and the idle edges after `HALT`. The counted coverage gate is satisfied (every opcode retires at least 200 times; every other bucket at least 10; the bucket table is in the console log). **Scope limits.** Programs are loaded over the real serial load phase and run from program memory; boot-ROM fetch, warm start and the SPI boot path (DR 0013 layer 2) are out of scope (`test_boot_rom.py`, `test_boot_spi.py`). The compare reads RTL internals, so gate-level lockstep is deferred, not done. RTL simulation only (Icarus + cocotb): no synthesis, timing or area number is claimed, from either flow. **Open details, not ratified behaviour.** DR 0001 is silent on the polarity of C after `SUB`; the model carries the RTL's documented borrow convention as an explicit, labelled pin (`isa.OPEN_DETAILS['sub-c-polarity']`), checked on its own by `test_sub_carry_polarity_pin`, so agreement there is an RTL pin matching the model's pin of the same choice and is not evidence that the polarity is correct. Three smaller details (SHF and Z, AND/OR/XOR and C, SHF fill bit) are named in `isa.OPEN_DETAILS` and also match the RTL. They are filed as issue #203. **No RTL/DR disagreement was found**; nothing was patched and no spec was relaxed. **Sensitivity.** 8 of 8 single-defect copies of the core (SUB carry polarity flipped, SHF direction swapped, BZ/BNZ swapped, WAIT off by one, ADD never sets C, AND clears C, HALT does not hold the PC, reserved IN port reads a pin) are each caught by the lockstep, the SUB one also by the polarity-pin test. **Cycle cross-check.** For all 400 programs the ISS's cost for every retired instruction equals the DR 0003 assembler's static cycle count, assembled words equal the generator's encoding (reserved no-ops excepted: the assembler refuses them), and each straight-line program's assembler `total_cycles` equals the ISS span; the RTL leg is the per-edge compare, which holds the same pc for pc.
