  - **What the fix is** (`src/pdn_cfg.tcl`, a custom `FP_PDN_CFG` replacing LibreLane's default `pdn_cfg.tcl`, plus the keys it needs in `src/config.json`):
    1. **Same-layer Metal4 bridges, drawn as fixed special-net boxes before pdngen runs**: one full-core-height stripe per macro power stripe, on that pin's globally-connected net, footprint-identical to the stripe. **56 bridges** in total — 16 `VDD!` stripes bridged *down* to the core bottom, 16 `VDDARRAY!` stripes bridged *up* to the core top (the macro's LEF obstructs Metal4 across the stripe x positions between those pins' y extents, so neither may cross that band), and 24 full-height bridges (20 `VSS!` + the 4 full-height periphery `VDD!` stripes). pdngen's `Metal1↔Metal4` connect then places the via stacks from the bridges to the standard-cell rails, which is what wires the macro into the core grid. This mechanism is used because pdngen cannot place these shapes itself: a core-grid strap at a stripe x is severed by the net-less LEF-OBS halo of the flanking stripes (verified on run 36773701780's step-19 ODB: zero straps survive over the macro at any width), and a macro-grid strap is claimed away entirely by the stdcell grid's domain obstruction (verified: `define_pdn_grid -macro` + `add_pdn_stripe` yields "does not contain any shapes or vias").
    2. **Core strap lattice re-pitched** `FP_PDN_VPITCH` 50.0 → **44.96** µm (= 4 × the macro's 11.24 µm stripe pitch) with `FP_PDN_VOFFSET` **93.985** so every core strap crossing the macro lands exactly on a same-net stripe (merging with its bridge) or in clear space. The template's 50.0 µm pitch would have placed its x=112.88/116.98 µm strap pair onto the macro's opposite-net stripes at x=112.32/117.94 µm — a 0.09 µm direct short against a full-height bridge (found by geometry audit before it could reach CI).
    3. **Power pins as two full-die-height block pins** (VPWR at x=366.625, VGND at x=370.725 µm, width 2.1 µm, correct sig types): the TT precheck requires every VPWR/VGND port rect in the streamed LEF to sit within 10 µm of *both* die edges, which pin promotion on the stdcell grid cannot satisfy once the macro splits the straps it promotes.
  - **AC2 — power grid clean**: `OpenROAD.GeneratePDN` logs `[INFO PSM-0040] All shapes on net VPWR are connected.` and the same for `VGND`; `design__power_grid_violation__count = 0` (per-net VPWR = 0, VGND = 0). `ERROR_ON_PDN_VIOLATIONS` untouched (still `true`).
