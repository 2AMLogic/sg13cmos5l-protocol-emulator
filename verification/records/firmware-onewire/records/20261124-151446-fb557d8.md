- **Claim**: the committed program `firmware/asm/onewire_master.asm` (image `firmware/build/onewire_master.hex`, 252 words of the 256-word store), loaded over the serial load-phase pins onto the RTL top and run by the ISA core, acts as a **1-Wire bus master** for one DS18B20 on an open-drain, pulled-up DQ line (`uio[3]`, DR 0010's `onewire` profile, `UIO_OD` = 0x08): reset + presence detect, Read ROM (8 bytes, Dallas CRC-8 checked in software), Skip ROM + Convert T with the read-slot conversion poll, reset, Skip ROM + Read Scratchpad (9 bytes, CRC-8 checked), with every received byte published on `uo_out` and a final status byte (1 ok, 2 no presence, 3 ROM CRC error, 4 scratchpad CRC error). The independent DS18B20 model `verification/reference_models/onewire.py` (written from the DS18B20 datasheet's 1-Wire standard-speed timing table and AN27's CRC alone; its `crc8` held to AN27's worked ROM example and the CRC-8/1-Wire check value by `verification/test_onewire_model.py`) decodes every slot, measures every reset / presence / write / read pulse in ns against the datasheet's numbers, and grades the master: the happy path runs with zero violations, the empty bus yields status 2, a corrupted scratchpad byte yields status 4 and a corrupted ROM CRC byte yields status 3, and five in-simulation negative controls (a reset ~5 us short of 480 us, a write-1 low of ~0.24 us, the scratchpad CRC check disabled, a read sampled late at 16.98 us after the falling edge, and a presence sampled late at 120.04 us after reset release) fail for their own reasons. The model drives worst-case slave timing (a presence pulse that starts 15 us after reset release and holds 60 us; a 0 bit held low 15 us), and the bench time-stamps every retired `IN R, UIO_IN`: the committed image samples presence at **70.04 us** after reset release (window 60..75 us) and data at **8.98 us** after the falling edge (limit 15 us) on all four read sites (presence, conversion poll, ROM, scratchpad). The bench fails a run whose core never reaches HALT, and the temperature decode sign-extends the 12-bit register (a -10.125 °C end-to-end run and a decode table). The silicon-true pad model (`verification/uio_pads.py`) resolves DQ and reports that the design drove exactly DQ, only ever low, with no contention. Firmware only, on the frozen ISA (DR 0017; no DR 0015 primitive, no RTL change); multi-protocol extra credit, the 1-Wire row of the catalog (#443, whose `docs/protocols.md` had not landed when this was recorded — the row text lives in `firmware/README.md`'s 1-Wire section pending #443). The 750 ms real conversion time is not simulated (the model answers the poll busy for a configured 3 read slots); every microsecond figure is core cycles × 20 ns at target-spec row 4's unconfirmed 50 MHz target; the pad model is zero-delay by design.
