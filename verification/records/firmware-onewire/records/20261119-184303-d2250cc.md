# firmware-onewire — 20261119-184303-d2250cc

- **Record ID**: 20261119-184303-d2250cc
- **Claim**: the committed program `firmware/asm/onewire_master.asm` (image `firmware/build/onewire_master.hex`, 252 words of the 256-word store), loaded over the serial load-phase pins onto the RTL top and run by the ISA core, acts as a **1-Wire bus master** for one DS18B20 on an open-drain, pulled-up DQ line (`uio[3]`, DR 0010's `onewire` profile, `UIO_OD` = 0x08): reset + presence detect, Read ROM (8 bytes, Dallas CRC-8 checked in software), Skip ROM + Convert T with the read-slot conversion poll, reset, Skip ROM + Read Scratchpad (9 bytes, CRC-8 checked), with every received byte published on `uo_out` and a final status byte (1 ok, 2 no presence, 3 ROM CRC error, 4 scratchpad CRC error). The independent DS18B20 model `verification/reference_models/onewire.py` (written from the DS18B20 datasheet's 1-Wire standard-speed timing table and AN27's CRC alone; its `crc8` held to AN27's worked ROM example and the CRC-8/1-Wire check value by `verification/test_onewire_model.py`) decodes every slot, measures every reset / presence / write / read pulse in ns against the datasheet's numbers, and grades the master: the happy path runs with zero violations, the empty bus yields status 2, a corrupted scratchpad byte yields status 4 and a corrupted ROM CRC byte yields status 3, and three in-simulation negative controls (a reset ~5 us short of 480 us, a write-1 low of ~0.24 us, and the scratchpad CRC check disabled) fail for their own reasons. The silicon-true pad model (`verification/uio_pads.py`) resolves DQ and reports that the design drove exactly DQ, only ever low, with no contention. Firmware only, on the frozen ISA (DR 0017; no DR 0015 primitive, no RTL change); multi-protocol extra credit, the 1-Wire row of the catalog (#443, whose `docs/protocols.md` had not landed when this was recorded — the row text lives in `firmware/README.md`'s 1-Wire section pending #443). The 750 ms real conversion time is not simulated (the model answers the poll busy for a configured 3 read slots); every microsecond figure is core cycles × 20 ns at target-spec row 4's unconfirmed 50 MHz target; the pad model is zero-delay by design.
