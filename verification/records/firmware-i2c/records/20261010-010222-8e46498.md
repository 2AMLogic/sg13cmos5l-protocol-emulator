- **Claim**: The committed I2C controller-write programs (`i2c_std`, `i2c_fast`, 182 words each, with the DR 0012 `WCTL UIO_OD` preamble) produce a conforming I2C transfer **on lines the design drives through its real `uio_oe`** (target-spec rows 1 and 12; DR 0012, Proposed), on the tree that also carries #162's refactored bench parsers and #169's boot ROM.
