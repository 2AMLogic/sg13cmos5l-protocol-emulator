- **Record ID**: 20261009-215218-8c2eb61
- **Claim**: The four committed I2C write / repeated-START / read programs (`i2c_{fast,std}_sr` exact and `i2c_{fast,std}_sr_poll` clock-stretch-aware) produce a conforming transfer **on lines the design drives through its real `uio_oe`**, including the two cases the old bench could not represent: the controller *releasing* SDA so the peripheral's read data is the line, and the peripheral *holding* SCL low while the controller has released it (target-spec rows 1 and 12; DR 0012, Proposed). Every run is on the silicon-true pad model `verification/uio_pads.py` (per `uio` pin: the design's `uio_oe`/`uio_out`, a pull-up on SCL = `uio[0]` and SDA = `uio[7]`, the peripheral as an external open-drain driver, resolved into one line that `uio_in` reads; the six other lines tied low). The peripheral watches the SCL line and drives through the pad model as an open-drain device; the independent `reference_models/i2c.py` grades SCL and SDA as sampled on `uio_in`; and every run -- stretched ones included -- ends with the check that the design enabled exactly the SCL and SDA pads, only ever drove them low, and never contended. **Why this record supersedes.** The superseded record's bus was composed by the bench: `line = uio_out AND uio_in`, with `uio_oe` never read (DR 0008 § Context). That composition is what an open-drain bus does *if* the pads implement open-drain, so it could not distinguish a chip that drives the line from one that cannot -- and it did not: the same composition passed on the pre-DR-0012 top, whose `uio_oe` was `8'h00` and which could not pull an I2C line low. The superseded record said as much ("no evidence that the pins are driven correctly ... pads are issue #136"). Its *numbers* are not corrected here -- they reproduce -- its *basis* is replaced: DR 0012 § Consequences and `spec/verification-plan.md` § 7 require the I2C evidence to run on a silicon-true pad model, and this run does. **What this record does not claim.** The pad model is zero-delay and logical: nothing here is evidence about pull-up rise time, pad propagation delay, drive strength or bus capacitance, and an I2C bus still needs external pull-ups this design does not provide. DR 0012 is **Proposed, not ratified**; this record makes no target-spec row met. The pad model's own evidence is `verification/records/uio-pad-model/records/20261009-215247-8c2eb61.md`.
