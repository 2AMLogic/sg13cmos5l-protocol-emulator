- **Claim**: The four committed I2C write / repeated-START / read programs (`i2c_{fast,std}_sr` exact and `i2c_{fast,std}_sr_poll` clock-stretch-aware) produce a conforming transfer **on lines the design drives through its real `uio_oe`**, including the two cases the bench-composed bus could not represent: the controller *releasing* SDA so the peripheral's read data is the line, and the peripheral *holding* SCL low while the controller has released it (target-spec rows 1 and 12; DR 0012, Proposed), on the tree that also carries #162's refactored bench parsers and #169's boot ROM. Every run is on the silicon-true pad model `verification/uio_pads.py` (per `uio` pin: the design's `uio_oe`/`uio_out`, a pull-up on SCL = `uio[0]` and SDA = `uio[7]`, the peripheral as an external open-drain driver, resolved into one line that `uio_in` reads; the six other lines tied low).
