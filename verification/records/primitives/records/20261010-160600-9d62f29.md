random messages; the bit step; an LFSR/PRBS leg (carry-less polynomial arithmetic as the oracle, and the ITU-T O.150 PRBS7 period of exactly 127 distinct non-zero states); register readback, the byte pointer and the width mask. **P2:** an independent receiver (NRZI decode + de-stuffing by the protocol's own rule, which flags a rule violation) decodes the captured line for USB (zero-toggles NRZI, stuff after 6 ones), HDLC (stuff after 5 ones) and CAN (stuff after 5 equal bits), and the decoded bits must equal the data presented, with the stuff slots the receiver found equal to the slots `LINE_STUF` flagged. **Fixed latency:** `WCTL CRC_BYTE` takes 9 cycles and every other new index 1, with the cycle count checked against the bench's own latency table for several data values (`test_fixed_latency_is_data_independent`), and the primitives touch neither Z nor C and leave the unassigned indices `0x1A`-`0x1F` as 1-cycle no-ops (`test_no_flag_side_effects_and_unassigned`). **Not claimed:** any frame-level USB, HDLC or CAN behaviour (the primitives know no frame), any firmware that uses them (DR 0015 schedules USB TX firmware after this), anything about area or timing (`synthesis-baseline`, `librelane-corner-timing`), the formal latency properties (`no-data-dependent-latency`, `pin-write-latency`), or gate level (`firmware-gate-level`).
