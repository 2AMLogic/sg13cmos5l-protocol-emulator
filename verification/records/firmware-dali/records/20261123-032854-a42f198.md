# DALI control-device firmware on the RTL

- **Record ID**: 20261123-032854-a42f198
- **Claim**: The 226-word image sends direct arc power to short address zero, queries its actual level, and preserves the last valid reply through malformed Manchester data edges and through a start low or final low of 18,600 cycles, which is 340 under the 18,940 minimum.
- **Design provenance**: Firmware, serial-load harness, gear model and functional SRAM RTL are byte-identical to record `20261121-083338-c11d39d` (every input hash in the record header matches), run on the merged tree at `a42f19835a288a54a0d84e38a8af48981b69ae58`. The simulated clock is the unconfirmed 50 MHz target.
- **Run configuration**: `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-dali.json --format json`, with the real serial loader and the simulator clock set to 20 ns.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed transactions using seed 1. The valid replies use half-bits of 18,940, 20,833 and 23,148 cycles.
- **Result**: Both cocotb cases passed (`transactions` in 274 s wall, `half_bit_mutant`). The gear decoded forwards `0x0054` and `0x01A0`. The 8,000-cycle late and early boundaries and the 3,000-cycle late mid-bit edge still preserved their held RESULT (`Rejected late_boundary/early_boundary/late_mid ... RESULT held 54` in the log). A start low and a final low before the stop, each cut to 18,600 cycles, left RESULT at `0xA7` at start-poll offsets of 0, 75, 150 and 225 cycles: eight `Rejected short_final on 82` / `Rejected short_start on 95` lines, all `RESULT held a7`. Valid replies with every half-bit at 18,940 cycles decoded on `0x82` at the same four offsets, and the following forward frame followed each. Backward EOF to the next query took at most 477,038 cycles for the minimum-rate gear (`reply 82, Te 18940`, phase 225) and 460,313 for the slowest (`reply 81, Te 23148, gap 22 Te`). The TX timing mutant failed its half-bit check at 13,232 cycles. All of these figures were read from the committed `test_icarus.log`.
