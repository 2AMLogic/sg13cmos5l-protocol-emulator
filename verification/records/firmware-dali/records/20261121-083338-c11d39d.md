# DALI control-device firmware on the RTL

- **Record ID**: 20261121-083338-c11d39d
- **Claim**: The 226-word image sends direct arc power to short address zero, queries its actual level, and preserves the last valid reply through malformed Manchester data edges and through a start low or final low of 18,600 cycles, which is 340 under the 18,940 minimum.
- **Design provenance**: Firmware, serial-load harness, gear model and functional SRAM RTL at `c11d39d7909b2197ac61b7a55e29cf418254a5f5`. The simulated clock is the unconfirmed 50 MHz target.
- **Run configuration**: `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-dali.json --format json`, with the real serial loader and the simulator clock set to 20 ns.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed transactions using seed 1. The valid replies use half-bits of 18,940, 20,833 and 23,148 cycles.
- **Result**: Both cocotb cases passed. The gear decoded forwards `0x0054` and `0x01A0`. The 8,000-cycle late and early boundaries and the 3,000-cycle late mid-bit edge still preserved their held RESULT. New: a start low and a final low before the stop, each cut to 18,600 cycles, left RESULT at `0xA7` at start-poll offsets of 0, 75, 150 and 225 cycles (eight rejections, on replies `0x95` and `0x82`). Valid replies with every half-bit at 18,940 cycles decoded on `0x82` at the same four offsets and on `0x95` at 150, and the following nominal reply published `0xA7`. Backward EOF to the next query took 477,038 cycles at most for the minimum-rate gear and 460,313 for the slowest. The TX timing mutant failed its half-bit check at 13,232 cycles. The same bench against the previous image (222 words, record `20261121-050713-754fa04`) failed at the first 18,600-cycle final low, with RESULT overwritten.
