# DALI control-device firmware on the RTL

- **Record ID**: 20261126-183357-3a3a165
- **Claim**: The serially loaded, 230-word image sends direct arc power to short address zero, queries its actual level, and publishes a valid reply after the backward stop bits. A single 18,600- or 23,250-cycle start low or final-zero low holds the prior RESULT. A subsequent valid reply publishes a different byte.
- **Design provenance**: Revision `3a3a1657204ad60eeae47e6ee4cb54432ccb0526` contains the revised phase bench and assembly timing comments. The hex and cycle report match the preceding record. The simulated core clock is the unconfirmed 50 MHz target.
- **Run configuration**: Ran `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-dali.json --format json` through the real serial loader. Gear transitions occur 1 ns after the clock edge. For a 23,250-cycle low, the bench records the detecting `IN`, the last guard sample, and the last rise sample relative to the pad fall, rounded up to whole cycles.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed transactions with seed 1. At a 12-Te reply gap, valid 18,940-cycle halves publish at offsets 0, 75, 150 and 225. Valid 20,833-cycle halves publish at offset 0. Valid 23,148-cycle replies ending in zero publish at offsets 0, 75, 150, 225, 1, 4, 14 and 1,978. The 18,600-cycle faults run at the original four offsets and the 23,250-cycle faults run at all eight.
- **Result**: Both `transactions` and `half_bit_mutant` passed. The four original offsets each gave three cycles to detect the start fall. The four added offsets gave 2, 24, 14 and 28 cycles respectively. At rollover the last guard and rise samples fell at cycles 18,688 and 23,205. On final zero the last rise sample followed detection by 23,168 cycles on the 24-cycle path and 23,164 on the 34-cycle path. The shortened TX half was rejected at 13,232 cycles.
- **klt provenance**: klayout-tools 0.9.2, commit `046ba74652fc096bd97071fc3237f7159dd40183`, Icarus Verilog 13.0 and cocotb 2.1.0.
