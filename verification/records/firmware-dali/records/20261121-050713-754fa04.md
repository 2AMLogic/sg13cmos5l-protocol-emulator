# DALI control-device firmware on the RTL

- **Record ID**: 20261121-050713-754fa04
- **Claim**: The 222-word image sends direct arc power to short address zero, queries its actual level, and preserves the last valid reply through malformed Manchester data edges.
- **Design provenance**: Firmware, serial-load harness, gear model and functional SRAM RTL at `754fa04c87b4992ed6b92fd20039cf8d2909cfac`. The simulated clock is the unconfirmed 50 MHz target.
- **Run configuration**: `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-dali.json --format json`, with the real serial loader and the simulator clock set to 20 ns.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed transactions using seed 1. The valid replies use half-bits of 18,940, 20,833 and 23,148 cycles.
- **Result**: Both cocotb cases passed. The gear decoded forwards `0x0054` and `0x01A0`. The two 8,000-cycle late boundaries on `0x81` and `0x7E`, an 8,000-cycle early boundary, and a 3,000-cycle late mid-bit edge all preserved `0x54`. The following nominal reply published `0xA7`. Backward EOF to the next query took 477,031 cycles for the fast gear, 471,904 nominal and 460,321 for the slow gear. The TX timing mutant failed its half-bit check at 13,232 cycles.
