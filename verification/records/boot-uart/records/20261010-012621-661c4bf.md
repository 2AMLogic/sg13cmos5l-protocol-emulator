- **Record ID**: 20261010-012621-661c4bf
- **Claim**: DR 0013 layer 2's UART load (`spec/decision-records/0013-program-loading.md`, **Proposed, not ratified**), as issue #139 scopes it, works on the RTL of the submitted top: **a PC on the demo board's USB-UART bridge pins (Tiny Tapeout option B: RX `ui_in[1]`, TX `uo_out[0]`) loads a program into a chip with nothing else attached, and the chip runs it only if it arrived intact.** Specifically, against an independent host model (`verification/uart_boot_host.py`, own CRC and own line timing, no import from `firmware/`): (a) a framed image (`0xA5`, `N-1`, `2N` bytes high first, CRC-16/XMODEM) at 434 cycles per bit is accepted, answered `0x06` + the chip's CRC (equal to `binascii.crc_hqx`'s), and run from address 0 with `R0`-`R3`, `PM_ADDR`, `UIO_DIR`, `UIO_OD` and `BOOT_STATUS` at their reset values, with program memory equal to the image word for word (RTL white-box); (b) the same at host bit-rate errors of -2 % and +2 % (target-spec row 10's bound), back to back and with idle bits, with edge jitter, and with the unrelated `ui_in` bits toggling, and a sweep past the bound that records where it stops (works to +5 % (host slow) and -4 % (host fast)); a host 12 % off in either direction is not accepted (the negative control); (c) a flipped payload bit (three positions), a bad CRC (high byte, low byte, both), a length too small (three variants) or too large, a wrong magic and a magic byte alone are never run: the reply is `0x15` or none, the core never leaves the boot ROM, `uio_oe` stays 0, `uo_out` stays at idle-high TX, and a correct frame sent afterwards is accepted; (d) garbage ahead of the magic is ignored; (e) end to end, the committed `uart_tx` program is loaded over the UART and its frames are decoded and graded by the existing independent `reference_models/uart.py` (`check_frame`, row 10); (f) a 256-word signed image (count byte `0xFF`) loads and then survives a warm start; (g) strap `11` and a failed warm start fall through to the loader; (h) the host model's frame equals `firmware/tools/loadseq.py`'s byte for byte for nine committed and constructed images, both agreeing with `binascii.crc_hqx`. Scope: RTL simulation; every figure is a core-cycle count and the clock is row 4's unconfirmed one, so 115,200 baud is arithmetic and not a measurement. Gate level is not in this record (the netlist of this revision does not exist before the `gds` workflow runs).

- **Result**: **PASS, 8/8**: `test_boot_image_chain_and_host_agreement` (20 ns), `test_nominal_load_runs_the_image` (9,014,620 ns), `test_row10_tolerance_edges` (125,149,100 ns), `test_bad_images_never_run` (154,130,700 ns), `test_garbage_before_the_magic_is_ignored` (7,625,820 ns), `test_end_to_end_protocol_program_over_uart` (6,936,220 ns), `test_full_image_and_warm_start` (45,179,620 ns), `test_fallthroughs_into_the_uart_load` (12,947,400 ns). **Tolerance:** the loader accepted every case at ±2 % and, in the sweep, works to +5 % (host slow) and -4 % (host fast) (back to back, a 22-word image, whole-percent steps); a host 12 % off was not accepted. These are measurements of this loader in RTL simulation, not specifications. **The reply** is decoded by `UartDecoder` at 434 cycles per bit, every edge inside a frame on a multiple of 434 cycles from its start edge, and `check_frame` passes each frame; reply bytes are 4,340 to 4,364 cycles apart (the stop bit is padded a few cycles past one bit). **Findings recorded in DR 0013, not worked around:** F2 the loader is 129 words and does not fit the proposed 128-word ROM cap (the generator's cap is now the 256-word address space); F3 it keeps a byte in `UIO_DIR` under a `UIO_OD`/`uio_out` guard, so `uio_out` reads 0xFF while it waits (`uio_oe` is 0 at every edge); F4 there is no timeout, and zero filler cannot resync it (a message followed by its CRC and zeros has CRC 0, so it is read as a longer valid image; a negative-control case found this); F5 `BOOT_STATUS` cannot tell a UART-loaded program from a warm-started one. **Negative controls: 7 of 7 caught, each by a test meant to catch it**: `verdict-always-accepts` (1 of 1 fail); `trailer-high-byte-unchecked` (1 of 1 fail); `crc-not-cleared-at-the-count` (1 of 1 fail); `rx-bit-period-wrong` (1 of 1 fail); `rx-reads-ui-in-bit-0` (1 of 1 fail); `tx-stop-bit-too-short` (1 of 1 fail); `handover-drops-the-guard-last` (1 of 1 fail).
