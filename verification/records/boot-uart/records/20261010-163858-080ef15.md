- **Record ID**: 20261010-163858-080ef15
- **Claim**: **The UART load (DR 0013 layer 2, strap `00`) does not detect every single-bit corruption of the frame's count byte; the claim "a bad length never reaches `RUN`" is therefore conditional, not unconditional (DR 0013 finding F8).** The valid two-word image `[0xF000, 0x13C1]` is framed `a5 01 f0 00 13 c1 00 00` (0x13C1 is the CRC-16/XMODEM of `f0 00`, so word 1 is word 0's own CRC). With bit 0 of the count flipped, `a5 00 f0 00 13 c1 00 00`, the RTL of the submitted top commits one word (0xF000), takes `13 c1` as the trailer, finds the CRC equal, replies `06 13 c1` and leaves the boot ROM (RUN). Program memory word 0 is 0xF000. The uncorrupted frame is accepted whole as a control. The count is not covered by the CRC, so a corrupted count is caught only when the CRC of the shorter prefix differs from the two bytes that follow it (probability about 1 in 65,536 for an arbitrary image, but 1 for this constructed one). This record pins the observed behaviour; it does not claim the behaviour is desirable and does not relax any spec.
