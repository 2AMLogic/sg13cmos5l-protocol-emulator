# Record 20261121-053414-a9c989e

- **Record ID**: 20261121-053414-a9c989e
- **Claim**: The committed CAN 2.0A image transmits a standard data frame
  (ID 0x5A5, DLC 3) at exactly 400 cycles/bit with its sample point at
  offset 300, arbitrated bit by bit with back-off and retransmission,
  receives a DLC-3 frame with de-stuffing and the remainder-zero CRC-15
  check, and drives the dominant ACK only when the CRC checked. Status
  words: 0x41/0x61 after the transmit, 0xC1/0xC3/0xC5 at HALT.
- **Design provenance**: RTL and the 253-word image at `a9c989e05c4e`. The
  source hashes above identify the RTL, the generator, the image, the
  assembler, the bench and the model.
- **Run configuration**: `klt functional-verification verification/request-firmware-can.json --format json`,
  using Icarus and cocotb. The simulation clock period is 20 ns (the
  unconfirmed row-4 nominal). No gate-level run is claimed.
- **Statistical convention**: Directed tests with seed 1, one committed
  program, fixed scenarios (clean TX, no-ACK, arbitration loss, RX+ACK,
  CRC-error RX, stuff-error RX, sample-point probes, bit-rate tolerance
  at +0.5% and +5%). Every negative control fails for its stated reason.
- **Result**: PASS, 9/9. The clean transmit decodes with zero model
  violations and every TXD edge on the 400-cycle grid; the lost
  arbitration (bit 9) leaves the winner's frame intact and the
  retransmission completes; the received frame is ACKed inside the ACK
  slot; corrupted CRC and stuff bits are rejected (0xC3/0xC5); a glitch
  straddling sample offset 300 loses the arbitration and one at offset
  100 does not; +0.5% bench pacing receives and +5% does not.
