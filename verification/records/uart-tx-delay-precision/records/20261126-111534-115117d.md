# UART TX path delay and cell-model precision

- **Record ID**: 20261126-111534-115117d
- **Claim**: Row 10's 0.0010 % maximum last-edge drift and ±1.25 ps mean-period deviation describe the frozen simulation with 10 ps cell-model precision.
- **Design provenance**: The routed netlist, final SDC and raw SDFs come from LibreLane GDS run 38136727636. Both IHP Verilog model hashes match the routed record. Both models declare `1ns/10ps`.
- **Run configuration**: Trace Q of flip-flop `_4500_` through the inverting B→Y arc of NAND2 `_2486_` and the inverting B1→Y arc of O21AI `_2489_` to `uo_out[0]`. Read the middle value of each unconditional SDF IOPATH triple in nanoseconds and convert to picoseconds. An output rise traverses a rise, fall and rise. An output fall traverses the opposite sequence. Clock insertion cancels in the difference.
- **Statistical convention**: Deterministic path sums, one per routed corner. The frozen UART measurements cover two bytes at each of three programmed bit periods per corner at a 20 ns clock.
- **Result**: The unrounded rise-minus-fall sums below reproduce the reviewer's independent 1 ps sensitivity run. At 1 Mbaud the slow-corner 13 ps magnitude is 0.0013 % of one bit. The 10 ps models instead produce signed residuals of +10, −10 and 0 ps. A 10 ps residual divided across nine bit intervals for 0x5a or eight for 0xa5 gives 1.11 or 1.25 ps of mean-period deviation. Both precision settings satisfy the 2 % TX bound for the measured frames. RX sample timing, the other TX rates and pad timing remain open.
- **klt provenance**: The routed record used klt 0.9.2. This static calculation reads the committed netlist and SDFs.
