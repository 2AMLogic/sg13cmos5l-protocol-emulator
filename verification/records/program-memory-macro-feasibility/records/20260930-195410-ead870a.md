    236.8 µm width exceeds a 1×1 die's 202.08 × 154.98 µm in either orientation
    (97.2 % of the 1×1 *core* area, and wider than the die). This is the
    load-bearing reason `info.yaml`'s `tiles` moves to `"2x2"` in this issue, ahead
    of any placed measurement — see `spec/gap-to-submission.md` row 7.
