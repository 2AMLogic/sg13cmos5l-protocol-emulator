  - **PARTIAL row-4 coverage — read this before reading the row as met.** The three
    corners above are the full set the LibreLane flow emits for this design (they are
    the `nom_*` corners `final-metrics.json` carries, and the same three
    `flow/run-post-layout-sdf.sh` already discovers from the flow's own `final/sdf/`
    output). `flow/run-sta-corner-sweep.sh` — the klt leg, and the thing that declared

    `slow_1p08V_125C`, `slow_1p35V_125C`, `typ_1p20V_25C`, `typ_1p50V_25C`,
    `fast_1p32V_m40C`, `fast_1p65V_m40C`. The three not covered here are the
    **high-voltage** halves of each PVT pair (1.35 V, 1.50 V, 1.65 V). This is a
    **structural PDK/macro limit, not an unmeasured gap and not a thing to re-run**:
    the program-memory macro ships exactly three liberty characterizations
    (`..._typ_1p20V_25C.lib`, `..._slow_1p08V_125C.lib`, `..._fast_1p32V_m55C.lib` —
    `src/config.json`'s own comment says so), and none of them is characterized above
    1.32 V. A six-corner sweep of *this* design could therefore only be formed by
    pairing a 1.35/1.50/1.65 V standard-cell corner against a macro characterized at a
    different supply voltage — which is not a corner, it is a voltage mismatch. No
    flow, klt or LibreLane, can close that gap for this design; only a differently
    characterized macro, or a different corner declaration, can.
